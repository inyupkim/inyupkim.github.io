Research

Monte Carlo Neutron Transport and Depletion

My work includes development and verification of depletion capabilities in the iMC Monte Carlo code, as well as variance estimation for depletion analysis using the improved deterministic truncation of Monte Carlo (iDTMC) method.

Related publications: Kim et al. (2025); Kim & Kim (2023); Kim et al. (2022).

Advanced Reactor and Multiphysics Analysis

My research also includes coupling the iMC Monte Carlo code with OpenFOAM for multiphysics calculations of molten salt reactors, and depletion studies of molten salt systems with online salt conditioning.

Related work: Kim et al. (2025); Korean Nuclear Society Autumn Meeting (2021).

Monte Carlo Uncertainty and Numerical Methods

Related collaborative publications address uncertainty quantification in quasi-static Monte Carlo transient simulation, kinetic-parameter evaluation using iterated fission probability, and history-based variance estimation in steady-state and time-dependent neutron transport.

Related publications: Oh et al. (2023); Oh et al. (2025); Oh et al. (2026).
